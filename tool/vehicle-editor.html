Bones
Automatic boxes
A set of boxes built off the model's own cubes: grouped by bone, cut wherever there is daylight straight through a group, and merged back together while merging is all but free. A draft to fit by hand — not a finished set.
Follow the model works the other way about: every cube starts as its own box and the cheapest pair is joined, over and over, until only the budget is left. It takes no notice of where one bone ends and the next begins, so it suits a machine whose bones are coarse. Trace the outline goes further: the model is burnt into cells and the cells are swept up into boxes, so nothing is ever enclosed that was not machine to begin with, and the budget is met by making the cells coarser rather than by boxing up thin air. Every box it makes is square to the machine.
